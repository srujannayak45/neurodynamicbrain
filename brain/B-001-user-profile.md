# Operator profile (example)

This is an example profile cell — the kind of "who am I serving" fact every brain should hold.
Replace the specifics with your own operator's working style.

**Working style (derived from analyzing a large sample of past prompts):**
- Senior platform/infra engineer. Operates across many repos: AWS + GCP, Terraform, containers,
  Kubernetes, gateway/substrate-style architectures.
- **Terse operator style** — short prompts (median ~11 words). Anchors well in real files/lines
  (strength). Fences scope hard ("only touch CSS", "you only do X, never Y") (strength).
- **Frequently dictates by voice** — the instruction can be buried mid-sentence after "uh/basically".
  When parsing a rambling prompt: extract the imperative, ignore filler, confirm your one-line read.
- Wants **token economy and speed** — prefers reusing cached knowledge (the brain) over re-deriving.

**Git identity:** commits under a project-scoped local identity, not the global personal one
(see [[B-003]]).

**Stack fluency:** strong TypeScript/Node/React/SQL; also Go, Python, Java, Terraform, k8s, AWS+GCP.
Multi-language, not single-stack. Skip beginner explanations. Welcomes long agentic runs.

**How to serve them well:** lead-verb interpretation; resolve pronouns ("the whole thing", "it",
"this") to concrete nouns before acting; bake verification into deploy/fire/apply tasks (verify-rate
is low relative to blast radius); give the *why* back to confirm intent. See [[B-002]].
